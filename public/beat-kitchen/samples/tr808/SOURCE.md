# Drum sample provenance

The ten WAV files in `public/samples/tr808` are a subset of [Michael Fischer's TR-808 sound sample set](https://github.com/tidalcycles/sounds-tr808-fischer), distributed by TidalCycles under CC0 1.0. The upstream LICENSE is included next to the samples.

| `1.wav` | `sd8/SD5050.WAV` | Snare |
| `2.wav` | `ch8/CH.WAV` | Closed hat |
| `3.wav` | `oh8/OH50.WAV` | Open hat |
